## SOAR preparation focus

As part of my preparation for open AI research opportunities such as EleutherAI's Summer of Open AI Research, I am using this repository to document my learning in areas that connect technical AI research with human understanding.

My current focus is on:

- information retrieval and RAG systems
- reasoning and evaluation in AI systems
- interpretability and AI safety
- human-centered evaluation of AI-generated explanations
- how users understand, trust, or question AI outputs

I am especially interested in how AI systems can provide answers that are not only correct, but also understandable, well-grounded, uncertainty-aware, and useful for real decision-making.
